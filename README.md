## Research

I study quantum-battery control through numerical simulation, including reinforcement-learning-based charging and energy-delivery timing, information updates for battery management, and observation-based correction of frequency drift in single-qubit charging.

I define the detailed research topics and carry out experiment design, implementation, evaluation, validation, and research documentation, with initial idea suggestions from my professor.

- [Quantum-Battery-Test](https://github.com/20252710/Quantum-Battery-Test) — An evaluation script for a PPO policy in a quantum-battery environment. The current public version contains the evaluation script; the environment implementation, trained model, and later research projects are not yet included.
